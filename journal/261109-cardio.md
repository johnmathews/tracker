# A third tracker: Cardio

On 2026-11-09 John tried three screens on round 2 of the fixed-week mockup
(`brand/design-system/mockups/261109-fixed-week`): "if i had three trackers - lets mock an extra one
up, for cardio (cycling and running), lets see how that works. can i scroll down twice". He then
asked for it to be built. His answers set its shape (K90):

- Cardio sits between Exercises and Food.
- Its items are things like cycling and running.
- It is marked like an exercise: great, partial or missed.
- Its chips are a time and/or a distance.
- It has no daily goal. Each item has a weekly goal, like the others.

## What changed

- **The registry.** `SECTIONS` is now `exercise`, `cardio`, `diet`, in the week's order, in
  `trackerTypes.ts` and `types.py`, with `fixtures/types/registry.json` keeping them in step.
  `pickKey` (`pick_key`) may be null: cardio's is. Cardio's item-page fields are Route and Link.
- **A cardio item** is `{ id, name, duration?, distance?, target }`.
  - `duration` is seconds in all, like an exercise's, shown with `durationText` ("30 min").
  - `distance` is km, rounded to 2 places like a weight ("5 km").
  - The limits are a day (86,400 s) and 1,000 km. Zero leaves either out.
  - The dialogs and the item page take Minutes and Distance in km (`useCardioForm`). Odd seconds
    show as decimal minutes ("30.5"), so a save never changes them.
- **The web app.** A `cardio` store, `CardioSection` and `EditCardioDialog`, modelled on Food's.
  - No Stepper on its title line, and no day turns green.
  - The arrows follow the order. Exercises has "Go to Cardio". Cardio has "Back to Exercises" on
    its title line and "Go to Food" at its foot. Food has "Back to Cardio".
  - `useScreenSprint` needed no change: it already finds every `data-planner-screen`.
  - `App.vue` only gained the section between the other two, to keep the merge with the keyboard
    shortcut work easy.
- **No defaults.** A week with no plan shows the default exercises and foods, but no cardio. An
  account from before Cardio loads with an empty section and an empty plan.
- **The cache went to `-v5`** (K14). A `-v4` tab doesn't know the section: it would write the
  cache back without Cardio, and drop queued cardio writes from the shared outbox. A `-v4` cache
  and outbox already hold item details, so they move as they are. Nothing is lifted, unlike `-v3`.
- **Export and import** carry a `cardio` section with no new version, as `goals` came. A file
  without one reads as no cardio.
- **The API.**
  - Migration 14 rebuilds `plans` and `ticks` without the CHECK on their section, as
    `item_details` already was (F3). The next tracker needs no rebuild.
  - `GET /api/state`, the export and the import gain `cardio`.
  - The insights, Ask and MCP tools take `cardio`. A cardio item's `seconds` and `distance_km`
    show in the week's items, so Ask can answer "how far do I run?". Its daily target is always
    null. Ask's prompt says only an exercise or food target being null means Daily goal is off.
  - `add_item` and `update_item` take `distance_km`. A field from another type is refused with
    the fields the type has.
- **Seed.** `tracker-admin seed-demo` adds Cycling (30 min) and Running (5 km), with marks, to
  the demo weeks that have no cardio plan, and a Route on Running.
- **The PlannerScreen card** describes three screens, and its preview draws them.

## Tests

- **Shared fixtures.** Every successful parse case now expects an empty `cardio` section.
  `parse/cardio.json` covers the rounding and caps. The insights fixtures add a cardio week with
  no daily target and an account with no cardio plan.
- **Web.** The cardio store, the section, its dialog, the item page's Minutes and Distance in km,
  the four arrows, a `-v4` cache moved to `-v5`, and cardio through sync.
- **e2e.** The mock state has Cycling and Running. `screens.spec.ts` now checks that no screen's
  foot rests inside the window, for each of the three screens. The fling and the wheel-turn
  tests land on Cardio, the next screen. An a11y test covers a cardio item's page and the Edit
  cardio dialog. These were not run here: the lead runs e2e after merging.
