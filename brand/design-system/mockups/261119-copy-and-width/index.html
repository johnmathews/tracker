Programmes: the copy landing, and the preview's width

Two questions from John's look at the build (#88). Each is answered on its own. Everything else on the Programmes page stays as it is.

  1. After Copy to library: the scroll, and how the copy lands in your library
  2. Preview and start (and the shared Preview): how wide the plan is on a wide screen

With reduced motion on, every option scrolls at once and shows its mark without moving or fading.

Question 1 of 2 · any user

1. The copy landing

Every option does the same first: after Copy (the row's question, or the Preview dialog's button), the page scrolls smoothly so the top of your library is in view, and the copy is first in it. Focus moves to the copy's name, so a screen reader reads where it went. The line "Strength 5×5 is in your library." goes, since the copy itself now shows it. The shared row's button still reads Added to library. Press Copy to library on Strength 5×5 in each stage, or Replay.

A. It slides in on a tile, then the tile fades

Option A

The new row slides down 8px into place and fades in over 240ms, on a tile background. It holds the tile for 1.5 seconds, then fades back to the page over 600ms. Recommended: the tile is the system's own "selected" ground, it shows where the copy is without a new colour, and it leaves nothing behind.

  • The row's words on the tile
  • Its facts on the tile

B. It fades in with an ink rule on its left

Option B

The new row fades in over 300ms with a 2px ink rule down its left edge, as the overview frames this week. The rule holds for two seconds, then fades over 600ms. Quieter than A; the rule is thin on a phone.

  • The rule

C. No motion but the scroll: a "Just added" Chip

Option C

The page scrolls, and the copy shows a Chip, "Just added", after its name for the rest of the visit. Nothing else moves. It stays readable after the moment has passed, at the cost of a word on the row.

  • The Chip

2. The preview's width on a wide screen

Today the open frame of Preview and start stops at 820px, whatever the screen, so the plan's columns squeeze and the rest scrolls sideways (John's screenshot: a third of the screen used). Each stage is a 1920-wide screen drawn at the pane's width, Base miles opened. The fields keep their width in every option; only the frame and its grid change. On a phone nothing changes: the frame already takes the whole width. The shared Preview dialog follows the same choice.

Today: 820px

For comparison

A. The open frame takes the page's whole width

Option A

Opened, the row's frame runs to the page's right margin, and the grid fills it: six weeks spread across the screen with no sideways scroll; a longer programme scrolls only past what fits. The closed rows keep their 820px, so reading the list doesn't change. The shared Preview dialog widens to the screen less 48px each side. Recommended: it uses the screen John has, at any size.

B. Wider, to 1280px

Option B

The frame and the dialog widen to at most 1280px: eight weeks fit at a readable width, and a very wide screen keeps some margin on the right. Columns stay closer together than in A.
