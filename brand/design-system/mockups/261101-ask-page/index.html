4. How plainly answers may judge (decided: V2, with streaks, scores and rankings)

The voice rules say “No guilt: never ‘You missed 3 days’”, “No streaks, scores or rankings”, and that the app never quotes a note back as advice. You asked whether you’re doing too much or too little, which needs a verdict. Two ways to write the same answer:

V1. As the rules stand: facts, then the next step

Pull-ups: three of four days this week, and four of four in two of the last four weeks.

Your goals mention ten pull-ups in a row by March. One more rep a set every second week would get there.

V2. A plain verdict, still calm (recommended)

A little too little for March. Three or four days of three reps is steady, but the reps haven’t gone up since 12 October.

One more rep a set every second week would get you to ten in a row by March.

V2 keeps the rest of the rules (no exclamation marks, no hype, no blame, no streak counts) but lets Ask say “too little” or “too much” when you ask, and quote your goals and notes back to you. It would be recorded as a carve-out for Ask in the voice rules.

And three short ones

  1. Keep a conversation across a reload? Recommended: yes, in this tab only (it’s gone when the tab closes, and never on the server). The alternative is to clear it on every reload.
  2. Import and your goals. Recommended: an imported file fills your goals only when they’re empty. Otherwise yours stay.
  3. Thirty questions a day each. Shown as “27 questions left today” under the box, and in words from three down. Keep the chip, or show it only near the end?
