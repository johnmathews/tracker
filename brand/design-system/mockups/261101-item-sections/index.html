Choosing the item page's sections

You asked to choose which sections the item page shows, and in what order. Each option here starts with Details hidden, as in your example. The question is where you make that choice. The page itself stays layout A (K65).

What is the same in every option:

  • Each section has a Show switch, and Move up and Move down buttons. Buttons move a section by keyboard and screen reader as well as by mouse; dragging could come later.
  • Changes apply at once, like the rest of the page. There is no Save.
  • The choice is made once for each type: every exercise's page shares one layout, and every food's page another. That is a proposal; the question that follows the pick asks about it.

Try it full size. The switch at the bottom left changes the option, the item, the state and the theme. The switches and the move buttons work.

A. Arrange on the page

Option A

An Arrange sections button at the top right of the page. Pressing it folds every section to its heading, with its switch and move buttons under the heading, and turns the button into Done. You see the new order in place. Recommended: the choice lives where it shows, and it reads like Edit plan does in the week.
