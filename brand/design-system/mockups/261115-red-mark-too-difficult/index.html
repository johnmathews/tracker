Red mark: too difficult

A red ✗ or a sad face reads as “didn’t happen”. A red day means you showed up and it was too difficult. This page decides the new red symbol for the tick sets and for the face sets, and shows the Appearance tab in its new order. Green, yellow, the squiggles, the pens and the box stay as they are.

Red days get new words too: a screen reader says “too difficult” where it said “couldn’t do it”.

1. The red tick (decided: A, one wave; round 3: the yellow circle is the wave too)

Ticks and Stamped ticks. Today the red mark is a cross. Both options are a hand-drawn wave at the tick’s marker width.

Now

A. One wave (~)

Option 1A

A single tilde across the middle of the box: up, down, and up again at the right. The plainest “~”, and nothing else in the sets looks like it.

B. Two waves (∿∿)

Option 1B

A longer wave with two humps, like a shaky line. It reads more as “struggled”, but at phone size it gets close to a squiggle.

2. The red face: the so-so face in red

Faces and Stamped faces. Today the red face is sad. John’s pick in round 2: the yellow so-so face’s straight mouth, drawn in red (C). The yellow and red faces then share a shape and differ only by colour; the screen reader still says “partly done” or “too difficult”. A and B, the open mouths, stay below for comparison.

Now

C. So-so face in red (:|)

Option 2C

The partly-done face’s straight mouth and eyes, in the red pen. Calm: “not today”, with no sadness and no surprise.

A. Round o

Option 2A

A small round mouth, the size of the so-so face’s mouth. It reads as “oof” or puffing hard: effort, not sadness.

B. Tall O

Option 2B

A taller, wider mouth, open wide. A stronger “that was hard”, though it can also read as surprise.

Week title, then Marks, then Theme. The controls and what they do are unchanged. The Marks tiles show 1A and 2C. The dashed outline marks the setting that moved and is not part of the design.
