# Red mark: too difficult

**Question:** which red symbol replaces the cross in Ticks and Stamped ticks and the sad face in
Faces and Stamped faces, now that a red day means "too difficult"; and the Appearance tab in its
new order (Week title, Marks, Theme). Green, yellow, the squiggles, the pens, the box and the
stamped look stay as they are.

**Status:** decided

**Link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/5ScUwNAtCa1eMLY6zmdsAW

## Options

1. The red tick
   - **1A.** One wave (~), a single tilde across the box. (Recommended: the plainest "~", and
     nothing else in the sets looks like it.)
   - **1B.** Two waves, a longer shaky line. Closer to a squiggle at phone size.
2. The red face
   - **2A.** Round o, a small round open mouth under the two eyes. (Recommended: reads as effort,
     "oof", rather than surprise.)
   - **2B.** Tall O, a taller, wider open mouth. Stronger, but can read as surprise.
   - **2C.** The so-so face in red (:|), John's own idea in round 2. Partial and too difficult
     then share a shape and differ by colour alone.
3. The Appearance tab: Week title, then Marks, then Theme. John asked for this order; shown, not
   an option.

The red day's words change from "couldn't do it" to "too difficult" (John, 2026-11-15).

## Decision

Decided 2026-11-15: **1A** (one wave), **2C** (the so-so face in red: "lets do a red version of
the current yellow :| face", kept after the colour-only trade-off was put to him), and the new
Appearance order. Recorded as K134 (wave and "too difficult"), K135 (red so-so face) and K136
(Week title, Marks, Theme) in `docs/decisions.md` §4.30. After trying it locally, John made the
yellow circle a yellow wave too (K137, round 3).
