A profile button in the bar, and a Profile page

The bar gets a profile button at its right end. It opens a small menu: who is signed in, then Profile and Sign out. Profile is a page like Settings. It holds the account and Sign out, your goals (moved from Settings), and a new box for what you have to work around. Ask and the Claude connections read both boxes.

Settings then keeps Appearance, Daily goal (the switch moves to a section of its own), Your data and Connections. The week, the bar’s other actions and Edit plan stay as they are.

One question: the button’s look. The frames are live: press the profile button to open and close its menu.

1. The profile button

Both options sit last in the bar, so it is the last Tab stop, and open the same menu under the button, right edges aligned. The bar is in view only in Edit plan, so that is where the button is.

A. An action like the others, named “Profile”

Option A

A BarAction: the Lucide circle-user-round icon over its name, the size of Settings beside it. It reads like the rest of the bar, and its name says what it is.
