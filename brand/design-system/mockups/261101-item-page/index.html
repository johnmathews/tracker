An item's own page

Pressing an exercise's or a food's name in the week opens its own page. The page holds four sections: Notes, Details for its type, This week's plan and Recent weeks. The question is how they are laid out. The page uses the Settings page's sizes (K51) and has no bar, like Settings (K52).

What is the same in every option:

  • The name in the week looks as it does now. It underlines under the pointer, and pressing it opens the page.
  • Notes and details are one copy for every week. Notes show as written. Edit notes turns them into a text box, and Done turns them back.
  • Fields save as you type, the way a mark saves when you press it. There is no Save button.
  • The plan section changes this week's plan, the same as the Edit dialog, and says so.
  • Recent weeks shows eight weeks, newest first. A week that met its goal shows its count in green, underlined, like a met day.
  • The field lists are a first guess: Equipment, Works and Link for an exercise; A serving and Examples for a food. They will change as we iterate.

Try it full size. The switch at the bottom left changes the option, the item and the theme. Everything on the page works.

A. Rows like Settings

Option A

Full width. Each section is a row: its heading in the 320u left column, like the week's names and Settings' headings, its content to the right, hairlines between rows. The order is Notes, Details, Plan, then Recent weeks. Recommended: it reads like the two pages you already know, and it has room for more sections as the page grows.

B. Notes as the page

Option B

Two columns under the name. The notes fill the wider left column, a step larger, like a page in a notebook. The plan, recent weeks and details sit in a narrower column on the right. On a phone the notes come first and the rest follows.
