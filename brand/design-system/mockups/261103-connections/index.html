Mockup · 2026-11-03 · Tracker

Connections: claude.ai sign-in and your tokens

Two new things. When you add the tracker in Claude by its address, Claude sends you to a tracker page that asks for your password and what Claude may do. And Settings gets a Connections row, where you see what’s connected, remove it, and create tokens for Claude Code and scripts. The sign-in card, the Settings frame and its other rows stay as they are.

  • Everything is drawn in the system’s tokens and components (the sign-in card, TextField, Switch, RadioCard, SegmentedControl, Banner, ConfirmDialog). The one new value is a monospace face for tokens and addresses.
  • Every frame can be pressed: Create token, Remove, the access switch, Connect when signed out.

Decided 2026-11-03: C2 (two choices on the consent screen), L1 (two lists in Settings), monospace for tokens and the address, and New with its banner. The token form keeps the SegmentedControl.

Try it full size. The switch at the bottom left changes the screen, the option, the state and the theme.

1. The consent screen (decided: C2)

Claude opens it in your browser. It’s the sign-in card: the warning first, then who is asking and where you go back to, then Email and Password. It asks for your password even when you’re signed in, so a link someone else sent can’t connect your account with one click. Connecting doesn’t sign you in to the tracker. Access starts as read only. The options differ only in how you choose access.

2. Settings › Connections (decided: L1)

A new row between Your data and Account. At its top: the address to paste into Claude, with Copy, and two lines on how to connect claude.ai and Claude Code. Then what’s connected. An app (signed in from Claude) lasts 90 days; a token works until you remove it. Each can be removed, after a confirm. A new token is shown once, with Copy. A connection made in the last day is marked New, with a banner, so one you didn’t make is easy to see.

L1. Two lists: Apps, then Tokens

Option L1

Each app or token is a row: its name, a line of facts under it, and Remove beside it, with hairlines between, like the rest of Settings. Create token sits under the tokens. Recommended: it reads like Settings, folds to a phone without change, and each list says what it’s for.

3. And three short ones

  1. The token’s access control. Create token uses the SegmentedControl, as Theme does (Read only | Read and change), whichever consent option you pick. Or should it use the same control as the consent screen?
  2. Monospace for tokens and the address. Recommended: the system font’s monospace (SF Mono on a Mac), only for those strings. The alternative is the sans, which makes l, I and 1 hard to tell apart in a token.
  3. “New” for a day. A connection made in the last 24 hours is marked New, with the banner on top until you dismiss it. Keep it, or drop the banner and keep only the mark?
