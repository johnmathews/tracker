Centred pages, and a tab for the shared programmes

Two questions from John's look at the build. Each is answered on its own.

  2. The shared programmes in a tab of their own, and its name

Question 1 of 2 · every page

1. A centred column

Today every page but the week runs from the screen's left margin, and its lists stop at 820px, so a wide screen leaves the right half empty. Each option puts the page in one centred column: Back to the week, the title, the tabs and everything under them. Lists and their rules fill the column; a paragraph still stops at 60 characters for reading. A preview's grid (K175) fills the column, not the screen. The column scales with the screen as the week's sizes do (u), so it is the same share of a laptop and a big monitor.

It applies to Programmes, a programme's overview, Settings, Profile, an item's page and the new item and new tracker pages. The week and Edit plan keep the screen's whole width: their grid is the screen. Ask keeps its side panel and centres its conversation as now. On a phone nothing changes.

Today: from the left, lists at 820px

For comparison

A. A centred column of 960u

Option A

960u: 960px on a 1280 laptop, about 1320px on a 1920 monitor. A row of buttons, a description and an eight-week grid all fit without stretching. Recommended: wide enough for a preview's grid, narrow enough that a list reads as one column.

B. A wider column of 1200u

Option B

1200u: nearly a 1280 laptop's whole width, about 1650px on a 1920 monitor. More room for a long programme's grid; on a monitor a list's rows grow long and the eye travels far from a name to its buttons.

C. A reading column of 760u

Option C

760u: about a paragraph's width. The calmest page; a grid wider than six weeks scrolls sideways inside it, as it does on an iPad.

Question 2 of 2 · the Programmes page

2. The shared programmes in a tab of their own

The shared programmes leave the foot of Library for a fourth tab, between Library and Add. Library is the account's own, as now. The new tab lists the shared programmes with Preview and Copy to library, under the line "Programmes anyone with an account can copy. A copy is yours to change and start." With none, it says "No shared programmes yet." After a copy, the Library tab opens with the copy landing first in it (K174). The question is the two tabs' names. Each option is drawn with the new tab open, on a phone (where four tabs must fit one line) and on a laptop.

A. Library and Shared

Option A

"Library" stays the account's own; "Shared" says where the other programmes come from. Four short tabs fit one line on a phone. Recommended: it keeps Library as yours and needs no new word.

B. Your library and Shared library

Option B

Both libraries named, each says whose it is. The longest pair: on a phone the four tabs take two lines.

C. Library and Catalogue

Option C

A catalogue is what you choose from; a library is what you keep. One word each, and it fits a phone.

D. Library and Templates

Option D

Says what they are for: starting points to copy and change. A word from software more than from training.
