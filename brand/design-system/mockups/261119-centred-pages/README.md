# Centred pages, and a tab for the shared programmes

**Question:** two questions from John's look at the build, each answered on its own. (1) Every page
but the week sits in one centred column: how wide. (2) The shared programmes move from the foot of
Library to a fourth tab: what the two library tabs are called.

**Status:** decided

**Link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/CR7UysZAsjSE5T7R2g2rVt

## Options

No option adds a colour. The column widths are the candidates.

### 1. A centred column

Programmes, a programme's overview, Settings, Profile, an item's page and the new item and new
tracker pages sit in one centred column. Lists and their rules fill it; a paragraph stops at 60
characters; a preview's grid (K175) fills the column. The week and Edit plan keep the screen's
width; Ask keeps its side panel. A phone is unchanged.

- **A.** 960u (960px on a 1280 laptop, about 1320px on a 1920 monitor). (Recommended: room for an
  eight-week grid, and a list still reads as one column.)
- **B.** 1200u.
- **C.** 760u, a reading column; wide grids scroll inside it.

### 2. The shared programmes' tab

A fourth tab between Library and Add lists the shared programmes with Preview and Copy to library.
After a copy, the Library tab opens with the copy landing first (K174). Empty: "No shared programmes
yet."

- **A.** Library and Shared. (Recommended: Library stays yours, no new word, fits a phone.)
- **B.** Your library and Shared library (two lines of tabs on a phone).
- **C.** Library and Catalogue.
- **D.** Library and Templates.

## Decision

John, 2026-11-19: 1. "A. 960u (Recommended)"; 2. "Library and Reference", his own
name, not one of the four offered. They became K176 and K177 in `docs/decisions.md`.
