A bar led by the week, and a smaller Ask page with two views

A long press already opens Edit plan, so the bar and Edit plan are now one thing. The bar drops its Edit plan button and the wordmark, and the week moves to the start as the page’s title. The Ask page gets smaller, reads its intro at a normal width, loses the “Ask” beside the question box, and offers a Document view (the default) and a Conversation view. The week grid, the bar’s other actions and Ask’s states stay as they are.

Four questions. Each picture is live: press the arrows, switch the Ask views.

3. The Ask page’s size

4. Document and Conversation

A switch at the end of the title line chooses the view: Document, the default, or Conversation. Each device remembers the choice. Conversation is one reading column, each question in a tile on the right, the answer under it (the 1 November mockup’s B). The question is what Document looks like. Shown at S2.
