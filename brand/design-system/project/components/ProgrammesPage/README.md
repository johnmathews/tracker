The Programmes page: the programmes running now, the library to start one from (with the shared library at its foot), and a way to add one, under three tabs.

## Use

- **Where:** `#/programmes`, opened from the bar (spec §9.1). It is a page like an item's page: no bar, Back to the week at the top, then "Programmes" in `title-1`.
- **Three tabs** (K147; K171 keeps three), the Tabs card: **Started**, **Library**, **Add** (K161: "Running" is also an activity's name, so the tab and a run's line say Started). The page opens on Started. A tab's content starts 8u under the tabs' rule.
- **Started:** one line per live run, each a link to its overview (`#/programme/<tracker>`, the ProgrammeGrid card). The tracker's name in weight 600 (22u), then one line in `body` `ink-secondary` with its first sentence in `ink` at 600: "**Week 4 of 8.** Running. Ends Sun 17 Jan." or "**Week 3 of 9.** Paused since Mon 7 Dec. A cold." A Lucide `chevron-right` in `ink-secondary` ends the line. Lines are separated by 1px `line` rules, with none above the first (the tabs' rule is there). Under the pointer the name underlines, 2px.
- **Library:** one entry per programme in the account's library. Its name in weight 600 (an `h2` under the page's `h1`, so the headings never skip a level; "Preview" inside it is an `h3`), its kind, weeks and activities in `body` `ink-secondary` ("Strength · 8 weeks · 5 activities"), its description in `body`, then its actions: **Preview and start** (secondary, Lucide `play`), **Edit** (quiet, `pencil`; spec R3 of `docs/specs/2026-11-20-programme-builder.md`), **Duplicate** (quiet, `copy`) and **Delete** (quiet, `trash-2`; it asks first, in the ConfirmDialog). Edit is a link to `#/programmes/<id>/edit`, the ProgrammeBuilder card; a programme saved there stays in its place in the list. Folded, Preview and start and Edit share the first row (Preview and start takes the room Edit leaves), and Duplicate and Delete share the next. An empty Library has one line in `body` `ink-secondary`: "The library is empty. Build one, or add one on the Add tab."
- **Opened in place to start** (K147): Preview and start opens the programme where it is in the list, in a box framed in 2px `ink` (`radius-lg`, 16px inside, 16px out past the list's edges; 8px folded). Its actions give way to:
  - **Name** (TextField, the programme's name to begin with; hint "Its tracker's name. You can change it later."), **Start** (a field-shaped date button with Lucide `calendar`; hint "Today, Wed 16 Dec, or any later day. Week 1 starts on its Monday.") and one TextField per parameter, labelled with its label and unit ("Hold start in seconds"), its hint giving the programme's own value ("The programme's own: 30."). The fields sit two to a row where there is room (at least 260u each).
  - **Preview** (`label`, weight 600), a line in `body` `ink-secondary` ("Six weeks, Mon 21 Dec to Sun 31 Jan. Week 6 is lighter."), then the ProgrammeGrid as the start preview: every week still to come, no week framed, no `tile`, no buttons. It follows each field as it changes.
  - **Cancel** (secondary) and **Start programme** (primary) at the end. Cancel closes the box and the entry's actions come back. Start programme starts the run and opens its overview. Only one programme is open at a time.
- **Reference** (K177, amending K171's option 1B; the shared library spec §7): a fourth tab, Started · Library · Reference · Add. Library is the account's own. Reference has the line "Programmes anyone with an account can copy. A copy is yours to change and start." in `body` `ink-secondary` over a `line` rule, then one row per shared programme, as a library row: its name (an `h2`, as the Library's), its kind, weeks and activities, its description, and two actions, **Preview** and **Copy to library** (secondary, Lucide `eye` and `copy-plus`; two to a row folded). A shared row names no one (S2). Copy to library asks first (K172), in a ConfirmDialog: "Copy Strength 5×5 to your library?", "The copy is yours to change and start.", **Copy**. When the library already has a programme of that name (case and spaces aside), the message is "Your library already has a programme called Strength 5×5. Copying adds another one." After a copy, the row's button reads **Added to library** (Lucide `check`) for the rest of the visit, Library opens, and the copy **lands** there (K174, option A of `261119-copy-and-width`): the page scrolls smoothly to it, first in the list, focus moves to its name, and the row slides down 8px and fades in over 240ms (`motion-safe:animate-land-in`) on a `tile` ground that runs 16px past its edges. The tile holds 1.5s, then fades over 600ms. With reduced motion the scroll is instant and the tile goes without fading. A failed copy or Preview says why in an InlineMessage (error) at the top of Reference. An empty shared library says "No reference programmes yet."; one that couldn't be read lists nothing under the line.
- **The admin's rows** (K171, option 2B): for an account with the admin flag, each own row ends with **More**, a quiet IconButton (Lucide `ellipsis`, named "More for Couch to 5K") at the row's button height, after Delete. Its Menu holds **Publish** (Lucide `share`), or for a shared row **Publish again** (`refresh-cw`) and **Unpublish** (`eye-off`). A shared row shows a "Published" Chip after its name, on the name's line. Folded, Duplicate, Delete and More share the second row, More at its own width, under Preview and start and Edit. Nobody else sees More or the chip; the server checks the flag on every publish (S1).
- **Width** (K175, K176): the page sits in the centred column, 960u wide on its 48u margins. The lists and their rules fill it, closed or open, so an opened row's grid fills the column (the start fields keep their own width, and a description stops at 60ch). On a phone nothing changes.
- **Preview** (K173, option C of `261119-shared-preview`): each shared row has **Preview** (secondary, Lucide `eye`) before Copy to library; folded, the two sit side by side. It opens the Dialog card, from 640px up the screen's width less 48px each side (K175), titled with the programme's name: the kind line in `body` `ink-secondary`, the description, the line "Eight weeks, at the programme's own values. Deload in weeks 4 and 8. You choose your own values and start day when you start your copy.", the ProgrammeGrid as the start's preview with `undated` (weeks by number), then each activity as Publish lists them. **Close** and **Copy to library** (primary, `copy-plus`) end it. Copy copies without the row's question, since the Dialog was the look, then closes it; the row's button then reads Added to library. When the library already has a programme of that name, an InlineMessage (info) above the buttons says "Your library already has a programme called Strength 5×5. Copying adds another one." A failed copy says why in the Dialog (InlineMessage, error) and keeps it open.
- **Publish** opens the Dialog card on the server's preview (S5), titled "Publish Hip mobility". Under "What everyone will see" (`label`, an `h3`), the programme sits on the page's `canvas` in a 1px `line` frame, `radius-md`: its name in weight 600, the kind line, the description, then each activity: its name in weight 600 with a Chip when its sets and reps are plain numbers ("5 × 5"), its notes as a note shows them (serif italic, markdown), or "No notes." in `caption` `ink-secondary`, and its link as a TextLink, "Open stronglifts.com in a new tab". Under the frame, "Everyone with an account can see and copy it. Your name is not shown." Then **Cancel** and **Publish programme** (primary), which sends the preview's `rev`. After it, an InlineMessage (success) at the top of the tab says "Hip mobility is in Reference.", and the row gains its chip.
  - **Publish again** opens the same Dialog titled "Publish Strength 5×5 again", first saying "It replaces the shared copy from Tue 17 Nov. Copies people have made stay as they are.", with **Publish again** as its primary.
  - A stale `rev` (the programme or its shared copy changed since the preview) shows the fresh preview with an InlineMessage (info): "The programme changed since this preview. Check it, then publish." A refusal shows an InlineMessage (error) over the actions, and the Dialog stays open.
  - **Unpublish** asks in the ConfirmDialog: "Unpublish Strength 5×5?" / "It leaves Reference. Copies people have made stay theirs." / Cancel, **Unpublish**. Focus returns to the row's More.
- **Add:** first **Build a programme** (primary, spec R2), a link to `#/programmes/new`, the ProgrammeBuilder card; folded, it fills the row. A programme built there and saved opens Library with its row landing, as a copy does (K174). A 1px `line` rule under it, then **Programme JSON**, TextArea's box seven lines tall in `body` at 17u, its hint "Paste a programme in the tracker-programme/1 format, or choose a file. Claude can add one for you through your connection." Then **Choose file** (secondary, Lucide `upload`) and **Check and add** (primary), which sends the text to the validator and adds the programme when it has no problems.
- **The validator's problems** (spec §3.4) show between the box and the buttons: an InlineMessage (error) with the count in words ("This programme has three problems. Fix them, then check it again."), then one line each in `caption`, its JSON path first in weight 600 and a colon: "**activities[2].weight.rate:** a progression has add or rate, not both." The box's border turns `error-ink`. The text the person pasted stays as it was.
- **Offline,** Preview and start, Duplicate, Delete, More, Copy to library and Check and add are disabled, with one InlineMessage (info) at the top of the tab saying why (spec §11.2). The lists still show what was last synced.
- **Sizes:** the ItemPage's at u = 1: text 19u, names 22u, controls 60u (the library's actions 52u). **Folded**, the system's n sizes: text 18n, labels and names 16n and 18n, controls 48px.

## Accessibility

- Tabs as the card: `role="tablist"`, one tab stop, the arrow keys move between tabs, and the panel is labelled by its tab.
- Each run is one link, so a screen reader reads its name and its line together.
- Preview and start has `aria-expanded`; on opening, focus moves to Name, and Cancel returns it to Preview and start. The open box is a `group` labelled by the programme's name.
- Reference is a tab panel like the others; its list is labelled "Reference" and the shared names are `h2`s, as the Library's are. After a copy, focus moves to the copy's name (an `h2` with `tabindex="-1"`), so a screen reader reads where it went (K174).
- More is named for its row ("More for Couch to 5K") and is the Menu card's trigger: `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded`, the arrow keys in the open menu. The Published chip is plain text after the name's heading.
- The Preview dialog is the Dialog card's, labelled by the programme's name; Preview says `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. Focus returns to Preview on Close and Escape.
- The Publish dialog is the Dialog card's: modal, labelled by its title, focus kept inside, Escape and Cancel close it, and focus returns to More. "What everyone will see" is a `section` labelled by its `h3`; the activities are an ordered list. The links open in a new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and say so.
- The preview grid is the ProgrammeGrid's: a real table in a focusable, labelled scrolling box ("Preview").
- The problems are tied to the box with `aria-describedby`, the box has `aria-invalid="true"`, and the InlineMessage is `role="alert"`, so the count is read out when it appears.
- Contrast, measured by the gate in both themes: `ink`, `ink-secondary` and `error-ink` on `canvas` and `panel`, `control-border` and `focus` on `canvas`; `ink` on `canvas` for the open box's frame; `ink-secondary` on `tile` for the Shared chip, `ink` on `tile` for More while open (the mockup `261119-shared-library` measured each).
